Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Board Games: Royal Game Night
📴 Works offline 🙅 No accounts 🔒 Zero tracking 📱 Data stays on device 🛡️ Family friendly
♛
The short version: Board Games collects nothing. Everything you do in the App stays strictly on your phone.

1Who we are

This policy explains how Board Games: Royal Game Night (the "App"), developed and provided by NG Studio, handles information. It works alongside our Terms & Conditions.

2What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. The App has no servers, no sign-in and no online features. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

  • No names, emails, phone numbers or contacts.
  • No location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases or payment details.

3What stays on your device

To make the games work, the App saves a few things in its private storage on your device only:

  • Profile: the player name and avatar you choose (you can use a nickname).
  • Settings: sound, music, volume, haptics, reduced motion and computer-player skill.
  • Saved matches: unfinished games so you can continue later.
  • Statistics: match history, wins and trophies, including the names typed in for each seat.

This information never leaves your device through the App. Other apps cannot read it.

4Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play. You can switch it off in Settings.

The App requests no internet, location, storage, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

5Device backups

If you have turned on Android's own backup feature, your device may include the App's settings and stats in your personal backup, under your Google account and Google's privacy terms. We never receive or see that backup.

6Your choices & deleting data

  • Change your name or avatar any time in Settings → Profile.
  • Erase history and trophies with Settings → Reset stats & trophies.
  • Remove unfinished matches with Settings → Clear saved games.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all of its data from your device.

7Children

The App is suitable for a general audience, including children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live).

8Links

"Rate the app" opens your device's app store, which is run by its own provider under its own privacy policy.

9Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that handles data differently, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
